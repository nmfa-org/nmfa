The alliance

Left alone, phases stratify. So do disciplines.

Multiphase flow runs through Norwegian research and industry — from subsea pipelines and process plants to hydropower sediment, nanofluid solar collectors, CO₂ transport, aquaculture, and the human body itself, in blood flow and drug delivery. Yet the people who study it, the people who engineer it, and the people who operate it often flow in separate layers.

The Norwegian Multiphase Flow Alliance keeps them mixing. We are a member network that connects Norwegian research groups, engineering companies, and industrial operators with each other and with international partners — for exchange, business, and complementary collaboration.

Subsea & pipelines Sediment & hydropower Separation & cyclones Nanofluids & solar thermal CO₂ & hydrogen transport Aquaculture & water treatment Hemodynamics & drug delivery

What we do

Four ways we keep the flow mixed.

Research exchange

Guest stays, shared lab and flow-loop access, and student mobility between member institutions in Norway and abroad.

Business & matchmaking

Structured introductions between technology providers, suppliers, and industrial end-users — from first meeting to pilot project.

Complementary collaboration

Consortium building for Research Council of Norway, EU, and international calls, matching members with complementary strengths.

Meetings & visibility

An annual forum, webinar series, and member directory that make your multiphase competence visible internationally.
